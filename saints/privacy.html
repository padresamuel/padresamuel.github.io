Privacy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

What never reaches us

The first names you type to make your family's calendar stay on your own phone or computer. They are not sent or stored anywhere.

What we keep if you give it to us

If you ask for the saint-day blessings, we keep your email, your family name, the saint days you chose (date and saint, without first names), the date you signed up and the video you came from. We use them only to send you a short blessing before those days, at most once a month. We never sell or share them.

They are stored in a private spreadsheet in the project's Google account (Google LLC is the storage provider). To stop the emails or delete your data, reply STOP to any email or write to padresamueloraciones@gmail.com.

Visits

We count visits per page and per day, without cookies and without personal data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical data such as your IP address.
